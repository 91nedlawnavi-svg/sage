# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Elliot is Sage's single user. He uses the browser chat on desktop and mobile for ordinary daily conversation, reflection, and practical help without needing technical knowledge.

## Product Purpose

Sage is Elliot's owned, persistent personal intelligence. Success means one dependable conversational presence whose local lifetime memory supplies useful continuity while models remain replaceable engines.

## Brand Personality

Calm, direct, and familiar. Sage should feel capable and present without becoming clinical, theatrical, generic, or noisy.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a sci-fi dashboard, admin console, engagement feed, glowing telemetry display, or decorative AI assistant. Avoid dense controls, visual novelty without purpose, and alarming treatment of recoverable failures.

## Design Principles

- Keep conversation primary and system state secondary.
- Make memory, model, and failure behavior honest without exposing implementation clutter.
- Prefer familiar native controls and plain language.
- Preserve continuity across desktop, mobile, keyboard, and assistive technology use.
- Keep risky actions distinct from routine reversible controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, preserve visible keyboard focus, use semantic controls and live regions, provide 44-pixel touch targets, support reduced motion, and keep mobile text inputs at least 16 pixels.
